About Ordelis

Ordelis is an India-focused cashback and rewards website. We help shoppers earn back part of what they spend at partner stores — with the rules written down in plain language.

What we do

A simple idea, run carefully

Online stores pay a referral commission to websites that send them customers. Ordelis is one of those websites, with one difference that matters: we share that commission with you.

When you click an Ordelis link and buy from a partner store, the store (or the affiliate network it works with) records that Ordelis referred the sale. After the store confirms your order, it pays us the commission, and we credit your share as cashback that you can withdraw in rupees.

We don’t sell products, take payments for stores, or ask you for money. Ordelis is free for members.

How we work

Our principles

Transparent

We show rates, exclusions and timelines before you shop, and explain every status change.

Honest

We list only active partnerships, never promise guaranteed earnings, and publish no invented reviews or numbers.

Careful with data

We collect what is needed to track and pay cashback, protect it, and never sell it.

Our promises

What Ordelis will never do

  • Charge you a fee to join or to withdraw confirmed cashback.
  • Ask for your card details, UPI PIN, OTPs or passwords — by email, phone, WhatsApp or anywhere else.
  • Promise a fixed income or guaranteed earnings.
  • Show fake offers, reviews, testimonials or statistics.
  • Sell or rent your personal data.

If anyone claiming to be Ordelis asks for a payment or an OTP, don’t share it — report it to support@ordelis.in.

Questions before you shop?

Write to us at support@ordelis.in. We reply to every message by email.
