How Ordelis works

Four steps, no guesswork

Every rupee of cashback follows the same path. Here is what happens after you click.

  1. 1

    Start at Ordelis

    Pick an offer and click through to the store. The visit must begin from the Ordelis link.

  2. 2

    Shop as usual

    Buy on the store’s own website or app in the same session. Ordelis never handles your payment.

  3. 3

    Store confirms

    Your cashback shows as Pending until the store’s return window ends and the order is approved.

  4. 4

    Withdraw

    Confirmed cashback can be paid to your UPI ID or bank account once you reach ₹100.

Helps your cashback track

  • Click through Ordelis right before you buy, and finish in the same session.
  • Pause ad blockers and allow cookies on the store’s site while shopping.
  • Use only coupons that the offer says are allowed.

Usually stops cashback

  • Returning, cancelling or exchanging the order.
  • Leaving and coming back to the store through another site or app.
  • Buying items the offer lists as excluded.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Ordelis free to use?

Yes. Joining Ordelis and withdrawing confirmed cashback is free. We never ask you to pay to unlock cashback.

How does Ordelis make money?

Partner stores pay Ordelis a commission, usually through affiliate networks, when someone buys after clicking an Ordelis link. We share part of that commission with you as cashback.

How long until my cashback is confirmed?

Stores confirm orders only after their return or cancellation window ends and they have checked the order. This usually takes 30 to 90 days, depending on the store.

Why might cashback be rejected?

The common reasons are a returned, cancelled or exchanged order, a coupon the store did not authorise, items excluded from the offer, or the purchase not being tracked because the visit did not start from an Ordelis link.

How do I receive my cashback?

Once your confirmed balance reaches ₹100, you can request a payout to your UPI ID or Indian bank account. We process requests within 7 working days.

Is any amount of cashback guaranteed?

No. Cashback depends on the store tracking and approving your order. We show each offer’s rate and conditions before you shop and explain every status so you always know where you stand.

Questions before you shop?

Write to us at support@ordelis.in. We reply to every message by email.
