Before you click

Check three things on every offer

Rates and rules are set by each store and can change. The offer card always shows the current details.

  • The rate. “Up to” means the highest rate in that store; individual categories can earn less.
  • The exclusions. Gift cards, some brands or categories, and unapproved coupons often don’t earn cashback.
  • The timing. Cashback stays Pending until the store confirms the order, usually 30 to 90 days.
