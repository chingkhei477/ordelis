Cookie Policy

What cookies are involved when you use Ordelis, and how to manage them.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

On this page

  2. What cookies are
  3. Cookies on ordelis.in
  4. Cookies set by stores and affiliate networks

Summary

The Ordelis website does not set any cookies, and it does not use analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking tools. It also does not store information in your browser’s local storage. Partner stores and affiliate networks do set cookies when you click through to them, and those cookies are what make cashback tracking possible.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. They can remember information such as items in a cart, or record that you arrived at a website from a particular link.

TypeUsed on ordelis.in?
Strictly necessary cookiesNo

If we add a feature that needs cookies — for example, a secure sign-in session or website analytics — we will update this policy before it goes live, and ask for your consent where the law requires.

Cookies set by stores and affiliate networks

When you click an offer, you are taken to the store’s website, often through an affiliate network. The store or network then sets its own cookies on its own domain to record that Ordelis referred you. This is how the store knows to pay a commission, and without it we cannot pay cashback.

These cookies are controlled by the store or network, not by Ordelis, and are covered by their own privacy and cookie policies.

Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block cookies on a store’s website, or use an ad blocker or privacy extension that removes them, your purchase may not be tracked and cashback may not be paid.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its “Last updated” date if our use of cookies changes. Questions: support@ordelis.in.
